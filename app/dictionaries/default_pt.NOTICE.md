# default_pt.json – Brazilian Portuguese word prediction dictionary

The 20,000 most frequent Brazilian Portuguese words, used for word prediction (suggestions while
building a sentence).

- Source: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, fork used by
  AsTeRICS Grid: https://github.com/klues/FrequencyWords), file `content/2016/pt_br/pt_br_50k.txt`,
  based on OpenSubtitles.
- License of the content: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Changes: only words made of Portuguese letters were kept; profanity, sexual terms, slurs and drug
  names were removed, because the app is used by children. Converted to the predictionary format.
